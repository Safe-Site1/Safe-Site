Safe Site AI Risk Assessment v3.3

Adds a permanent AI audit trail without changing the database schema.

For each submitted AI-assisted Pre-Task Risk Assessment, safety_records.data.aiAudit stores:
- original AI hazards and controls
- original AI risk suggestions
- AI generation timestamp/source
- supervisor review completion timestamp
- every stop-work threshold event
- final supervisor-reviewed hazards and controls
- final initial/residual ratings
- final residual score and configured stop-work threshold
- supervisor and crew acknowledgement

Safe Site also attempts a compact audit_log entry linked to the safety record.
If audit_log insertion is unavailable, the full audit still remains in safety_records.data.aiAudit.

Existing v3.2 supervisor-review and residual-risk gates remain active.

Replace ai-risk-assessment.js in GitHub and commit to main.
