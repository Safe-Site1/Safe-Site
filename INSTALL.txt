Safe Site AI Risk Assessment v3.2

Adds a residual-risk stop-work gate.

Behavior:
- Supervisor AI review remains mandatory.
- Residual risk at/above the configured site threshold disables Submit.
- The screen shows STOP — additional controls required.
- Changing controls or residual risk requires the supervisor to reconfirm the controls/risk review.
- A second click/function guard prevents bypass.
- Default threshold is 10 only for the current demo.
- Site configuration can override with:
  db.settings.riskStopWorkThreshold
  or window.SAFE_SITE_RISK_CONFIG.stopWorkThreshold

Before real-world deployment, configure the approved company/site risk matrix and stop-work threshold.

Replace ai-risk-assessment.js in GitHub and commit to main.
